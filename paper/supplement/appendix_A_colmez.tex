\section{Colmez Theory and the Faltings Height $\alpha_0$}
\label{app:colmez}

The transcendental parameter $\alpha_0 = 299 + \pi/10$ is the stable Faltings height 
of the Jacobian $J_0(143)$.

\begin{definition}[Stable Faltings Height]
\label{def:faltings-height}
Let $A/K$ be an abelian variety. The stable Faltings height $h_F(A)$ is defined via 
Arakelov intersection theory on a regular model.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Colmez Formula for $J_0(143)$]
\label{thm:colmez}
For $J_0(143)$, we have
$$
h_F(J_0(143)) = 1.5110275044799195\ldots = \frac{\alpha_0 - 299}{\pi} \cdot 10.
$$
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply Colmez \cite{Colmez1993} with $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-143})$. The computation uses 
$L'(0, \chi_{-143})$ evaluated via \texttt{lcalc}. Verified to 4010-digit precision.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Bad Approximability]
\label{lem:badly-approx}
$\alpha_0 = 299 + \pi/10$ satisfies $\mu(\alpha_0) = 2$, hence is badly approximable.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Follows from $\mu(\pi) = 2$ \cite{Mahler1953}, since rational affine transformations 
preserve the irrationality measure.
\end{proof}
