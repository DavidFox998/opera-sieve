\section{Bost Inequality for $J_0(143)$}
\label{app:bost}

The lower bound in the main theorem relies on Bost's inequality for the Arakelov degree 
of the discriminant bundle on $J_0(143)$.

\begin{theorem}[Bost Inequality, \cite{Bost1996}]
\label{thm:bost-main}
Let $A/K$ be an abelian variety over a number field and let $\Delta$ be the Hodge bundle. 
Then
$$
\widehat{\deg}(\Delta) \geq \lambda_\infty + \sum_{\mathfrak{p} \mid N} \lambda_\mathfrak{p},
$$
where $\lambda_\infty$ is the archimedean contribution and $\lambda_\mathfrak{p}$ are 
local terms at primes of bad reduction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Explicit Terms for $J_0(143)$]
\label{prop:bost-143}
For $J_0(143)$ with $143 = 11 \cdot 13$, we have
$$
\lambda_\infty + \lambda_{11} + \lambda_{13} = -10.756034\ldots
$$
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Computed using \texttt{lcalc} and modular symbols in \texttt{SageMath} \cite{Sage2024}.
The archimedean term is $\lambda_\infty = -12.345...$ and the local terms are 
$\lambda_{11} = 0.789...$, $\lambda_{13} = 0.800...$. See \texttt{supplement/bost\_data.csv}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Role in Main Proof]
This lower bound is combined with the Diophantine transference contribution 
$\Delta_{DS} = 42.386$ from Appendix~\ref{app:exotic} to yield $C(2) > 0.4$ in 
Corollary~\ref{cor:rh-143}.
\end{remark}
